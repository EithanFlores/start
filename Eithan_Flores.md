# What I think about Github:

I'm really confused when it comes to understanding GitHub and programming. It's one of those subjects that really interests me, knowing how imperative it is in collaboration, building projects, and, honestly, just to survive in this world in a competitive way. I respect how powerful the tools are; however, anytime I want to dive in, I end up being submerged by the complexity.

The really hard part for me has been learning from videos and written tutorials. They explain the concepts, but sometimes I find it hard to connect theory to practice. For instance, GitHub feels like this endless maze of commands, branches, and repositories that I just can't seem to wrap my head around. And programming—with its different languages and rules of syntax—seems to be learning a whole new way of thinking.
